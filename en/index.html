Publication · 2026

Civil law responses to deepfakes: honour, privacy and one’s own image in the digital age

An article inspired by an undergraduate dissertation, published open access on Zenodo.

Key legal conclusions

  • Spanish civil law and Organic Law 1/1982 can treat non-consensual creation or circulation as an unlawful interference with honour, privacy and one’s own image.
  • Plausibility, virality and persistence intensify moral, psychological and economic harm while making proof, removal and redress harder.
  • Civil protection needs stronger prevention, digital traceability, platform cooperation, updated rules and international coordination.
